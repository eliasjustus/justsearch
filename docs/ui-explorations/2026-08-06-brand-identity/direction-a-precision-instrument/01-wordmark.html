SHEET 01/05 — WORDMARK & MARK

The mark is a container, not a lens.

A magnifying glass advertises the commodity. This mark advertises the differentiator: a machined, closed body with the evidence cut into it. Nothing is drawn leaving the boundary, because nothing does.

The mark — “Enclosure”

Solid mass = your machine. The chamfer is an orientation key, the way a machined part or a memory chip is keyed — it says “a real object with a correct way up,” not “a friendly rounded app.” Cut into it: a datum bar and, set off from it, two rules of descending length — a margin and its entries, ranked. Evidence, held inside the body. Nothing touches the outer wall. The shape survives 16 px because it is a mass, not an outline.

Construction — 32-unit field, even coordinates only

GridEvery coordinate in the path is an even number, so at 16 px every edge lands on a whole device pixel — the mark never needs a hinted variant. The recurring measure is 4 units: the wall, the gaps, the bar widths.
ColourMonochrome, always. The mark takes the ink of its context; it is never filled with the accent.
Clear spaceHalf the body height on all four sides. The chamfer counts as body.

The wordmark — nameplate cut

CAPS · TRACKING +0.185 EM · WEIGHT BREAK 650 / 300 · NO WORD SPACE

The weight break is the space. Set as one uninterrupted word at nameplate tracking, the way equipment is badged — then the shift from 650 to 300 lets Just stand up on its own, so the three readings stay visible: merely search, simply search, and just as in justice. No colour is needed to do it, which is the whole argument of this direction: typography carries the brand, colour carries function.
