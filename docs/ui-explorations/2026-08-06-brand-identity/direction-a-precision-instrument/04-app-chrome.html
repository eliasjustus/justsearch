A white instrument, not a dark terminal.

The riskiest decision in this direction, taken on purpose: the app is enamel-white. Developers read it as tooling either way; lawyers, therapists and journalists read a dark terminal as risk. Density is unchanged — hairlines and surface steps do the separating that shadows and glass would otherwise do. Demo data below is fictional.

Elevation

Four surface steps, zero shadows. The rail sinks to #E7E7E3, the list sits on #FAFAF8, the selected row and the answer panel rise to white. Depth is value, plus one hairline.

Where the accent is allowed

Four places only: the caret, the focused field’s edge, the selected row’s left rule, and a citation marker. Never a fill, never a gradient, never the mark.

Machine facts stay mono

Paths, scores, counts, the port. Scores are tabular so the decimal column reads down; the status bar states the binding address because that is the promise being kept.
