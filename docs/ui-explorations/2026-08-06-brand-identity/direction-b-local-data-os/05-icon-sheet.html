05 — Icon sheet

Two drawings, five sizes

The mark survives to 16px because the wall carries recognition on its own. Below 24px it switches to an optical cut — a heavier wall and the arrowhead removed — so the hook stays a hook instead of turning into a smudge.

Why the cut changes. At 16px the 45° arrowhead falls below one device pixel per leg and fills in. Removing it leaves a clean L-hook inside the wall — still a return, still nothing touching the boundary, and it hints rather than mushes.

C

Application icon treatments

Transparent — primary
Panel tile — taskbar, tray
Signal tile — installer, splash
Inverted — light desktops

The transparent cut ships as the app icon; the panel tile exists only for surfaces that composite over unknown wallpaper. No tile is ever a gradient — the backdrop is one flat ramp step or nothing.
