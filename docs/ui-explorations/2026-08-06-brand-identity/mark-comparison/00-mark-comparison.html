Five marks, one presentation

Each direction's hero mark, lifted out of its own art direction and shown under identical conditions: same grounds, same sizes, same order, same chrome. Geometry is copied verbatim from the source sheets — nothing has been redrawn. The only edit is recolouring, and only in the two neutral columns.

Columns 1–2: the mark in its own source colours, on white and on #0e0f12. Columns 3–4: the same geometry flattened to one value — shape alone. Sizes: 96 px · 32 px · 16 px.

Direction A · Precision Instrument

“Enclosure”

A machined, closed body with the evidence cut into it — a datum bar and two rules of descending length, ranked; the chamfer is an orientation key, and nothing is drawn leaving the boundary.

Source: direction-a-precision-instrument/01-wordmark.html — line 135 (hero “The mark — Enclosure”) Small size: no dedicated optical variant — the source ships one path for every size (all coordinates even, so 16 px edges land on whole device pixels).

Direction B · Local Data OS

“The Chamber & the Return”

A carriage return sealed inside an unbroken wall: the query goes in, the answer comes back, and neither leaves the machine — a keyboard glyph read as “run this”, set inside a boundary read as “closed”.

Direction C · Evidence Room

“The Kept Record”

A bracket — the punctuation of citation — thickened into a binding on the left and a shelf along the foot, with the lines of a record nested inside; the last line, the cited one, is set in oxblood, and the corner stays open because a kept record is still being added to.

Source: direction-c-evidence-room/01-wordmark.html — line 255 (hero “The mark”) Small size: dedicated variant — the source shows a “Small size (≤32 px)” cut at 01-wordmark.html line 280, and a pixel-fitted 16-unit cut at 05-icon-sheet.html line 273 (three lines reduced to two). The 16-unit cut is used below at both 32 px and 16 px, as the source does.

Direction D · Sovereign

The parcel & monument mark

A cadastral parcel with a survey monument set inside it: one closed boundary line with no gate, deliberately stepped and out of square so it reads as a specific piece of surveyed ground rather than a generic UI frame, and the plat diamond as the fixed point everything is measured from.

Source: direction-d-sovereign/01-wordmark.html — line 129 (“01.1 — The mark, alone”, 188 px) Small size: dedicated optical variant — 05-icon-sheet.html line 131: the stepped boundary squared to the pixel grid, heavier stroke, larger monument. Used below at 32 px and 16 px, as the source does. Note: this mark is specified never to invert — it keeps its own dark ground on any surround, so columns 1 and 2 are intentionally identical.

Mark E · authored after A–D

“The Slot”

A sealed mass — your files, your machine — with one slot cut through its wall and a single line of text standing outside it; at display sizes that answer-line carries a footnote marker, because what leaves, leaves cited.

Source: mark-e-claude/01-claude-mark.html — lines 63–65 (hero, 96 px: slotted-mass path, answer-line rect x46 y28 w14 h8, footnote rect x58 y20 w4 h4) Small size: dedicated 16-unit cut at line 107 — slot deepened, footnote dropped. Used below at 16 px; at 32 px the source keeps the 64-unit geometry with the footnote marker dropped (line 103), so that is what is shown. Footnote marker: present at 96 px only, per the source's own size rules.
