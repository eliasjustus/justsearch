Two drawings, one mark. At 64 px and above the parcel keeps its surveyed irregularity — the edges are off-axis by a degree or so, which is what makes it a plot rather than a frame. At 32 px and below that irregularity turns to mush, so the optical variant snaps every edge to the pixel grid, thickens the boundary and enlarges the monument. Neither variant inverts: the parcel carries its own dark ground onto the paper, which is why the mark reads identically in both strips and needs no light-mode file.
