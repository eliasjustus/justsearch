Direction D — Sovereign Sheet 01 · Mark & wordmark
Inside the boundary

Your machine is territory. The product is the border around it.

Every surface in this direction is drawn as a surveyed plot: a hard line, monuments at the corners, hatch outside, clean ground inside. Nothing about the identity depicts searching. It depicts containment — the thing that is actually true and actually rare.

01.1 — The mark, alone

—— boundary · one closed line, no gate
—— the step · a real plot is never a square
◆ monument · what the boundary holds
   ground · stays dark on any surround

Rationale. A cadastral parcel with a survey monument set inside it. The outline is deliberately stepped and slightly out of square — that irregularity is the whole tell: a perfect rectangle reads as a generic UI frame, an irregular closed polygon reads as a specific piece of surveyed ground. The diamond is the standard plat symbol for a monument: the fixed point everything else is measured from. No lock, no shield, no keyhole — the security is in the geometry, not in a metaphor about guarding.

01.2 — Primary lockup

JustSearch
Retrieval that never leaves the machine

Rationale. Set in a gothic — the face of survey sheets, land registers and public notices, not of software marketing. The two-tone split does the work a separator would do without breaking the name: JUST in brass carries all three readings the name is built on (merely / simply / justice), SEARCH in bone is the commodity half, deliberately the quieter one. Mark and wordmark are divided by a rule, never merged — a boundary separates them too.

01.3 — Stacked lockup, and the same lockup off its ground

JustSearch
Sheet mark · 1:1

The stacked form sits on its own surveyed baseline: an oxide rule the exact width of the wordmark, monumented at both ends. Vertical use only where horizontal will not fit.

JustSearch
On paper · 1:1

The mark never inverts. On paper, in print, on a white doc site, the parcel keeps its own dark ground and its own boundary colour. The surround changes; the territory does not. The wordmark shifts to ink and a darker brass to hold contrast.

01.4 — Registry variant (EU / legal contexts)

JustSearch
Restricted use — contracts, filings, EU-sovereignty material
JustSearch
Minimum size 18 px · below that, wordmark only

Rationale. One sanctioned variant, for the audience that reads deeds for a living: Just set in the registry serif — italic, lowercase, the typographic register of a legal instrument — against the gothic SEARCH. It makes the third reading of the name explicit without ever writing the word "justice" down, which would be a claim the product cannot back.
